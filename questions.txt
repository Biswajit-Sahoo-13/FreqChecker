# FreqChecker: Design Review & Operational Questions
# Created: 2026-08-19

1. Executable Name & Location:
   - Application named `FreqChecker` (`freqchecker.exe`).
   - Bundled completely into the standalone folder `freqchecker/`.

2. Default Tone Duration:
   - Default is set to 2.0 seconds with 50ms raised-cosine fade ramps to prevent listener fatigue (reducing test time from ~15min down to ~5-8min), with user-configurable duration in settings and an instant 'R' replay hotkey.

3. Low-Frequency Roll-Off Baseline:
   - Weakness <= 160 Hz that recovers by 200-250 Hz is recognized as standard laptop physical driver acoustic limitation rather than hardware failure.

4. False-Abort Protection:
   - Global abort check excludes sub-200 Hz roll-off frequencies to ensure healthy laptop speakers with normal bass roll-off are never falsely aborted as defective.

5. Sequential Cross-Channel Testing:
   - Testing Left speaker followed by Right speaker allows full differential analysis to isolate one-sided driver problems from symmetrical DSP/room anomalies.
